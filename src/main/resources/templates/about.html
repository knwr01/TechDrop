About TechDrop

TechDrop is an online electronics store where customers can browse products by category, search for what they need, add items to a cart, place orders and leave reviews. This project was built for our Web Application Development course at Humber College.

Our goal is to make it simple and quick to find the phones, laptops and audio gear you are looking for, all in one place.
